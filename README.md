Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects
Focused on Pipeline Integrity, Asset Integrity, Inspection Management, Corrosion Monitoring, Inline Inspection, Risk Assessment, GIS, Maintenance & Reliability Engineering
Last updated: September 2026

This repository tracks notable SaaS/hosted platforms and open-source projects for Pipeline Integrity Management (PIM). These tools help pipeline operators and asset-intensive organizations manage pipeline assets, inspection records, inline inspection (ILI) data, corrosion threats, cathodic protection, risk assessments, maintenance activities, regulatory compliance, spatial data, and integrity-management programs.

Typical capabilities include pipeline inventory management, GIS mapping, linear referencing, ILI data management, corrosion assessment, fitness-for-service calculations, risk-based inspection, anomaly assessment, threat identification, maintenance planning, work management, condition monitoring, digital twins, document management, regulatory reporting, and lifecycle asset management.

Examples include ROSEN Integrity Solutions, NDT Global, PipelineManager, DNV Synergi Pipeline, Enbridge Asset Suite, Quorum Pipeline Management, OneSoft, Cenosco IMS, Inspectahire, Asset Guardian, Pipeline360, OneBridge Solutions, Enbridge PODS, Asset Integrity Engineering, Orbital Eye, and Hexagon SDx.

Open-source emphasis: This section is heavily expanded with open-source pipeline integrity libraries, infrastructure asset-management platforms, CMMS systems, GIS platforms, spatial databases, inspection-management tools, engineering calculation libraries, data standards, digital-twin components, machine-learning projects, workflow engines, and analytics frameworks. There are relatively few complete turnkey open-source replacements for enterprise pipeline-integrity suites, but a powerful modular ecosystem exists for building transparent and self-hosted pipeline integrity platforms.

Open-source Python library for calculating the degree of danger associated with pipeline metal-loss defects using the ASME B31G method. Useful as a building block for corrosion and anomaly-assessment workflows.

Open-source machine-learning demonstrator for predicting external corrosion on oil and gas pipelines using inspection and pipeline-related data.

Additional Strong Open-Source Options

Pipeline integrity calculations: PipelineIntegrity and other engineering Python libraries can provide transparent implementations of defect assessment, corrosion calculations, and fitness-for-service workflows.

Pipeline data architecture: PODS provides a widely recognized industry data model for pipeline assets, inspections, ILI data, cathodic protection, integrity, regulatory information, and geospatial records.

GIS and linear infrastructure: QGIS, GRASS GIS, GeoServer, PostGIS, GeoNode, GDAL, MapStore, OpenLayers, and Leaflet provide a strong foundation for self-hosted pipeline mapping and spatial integrity analysis.

Asset and maintenance management: openMAINT, CMDBuild, Atlas CMMS, ERPNext, Odoo Community, GLPI, and Snipe-IT can provide asset records, maintenance workflows, work orders, inspections, inventory, and lifecycle management.

Inspection and field data: QGIS field-data workflows, custom mobile applications, Node-RED, PostgreSQL/PostGIS, and document-management platforms can be combined to create open inspection-management systems.

Document and evidence management: Mayan EDMS, Paperless-ngx, OpenKM Community, and other open-source document systems can store inspection reports, test certificates, photographs, engineering documents, and regulatory evidence.

IoT and condition monitoring: ThingsBoard, Node-RED, Eclipse Ditto, OpenRemote, FIWARE, InfluxDB, TimescaleDB, and Apache Kafka can support telemetry ingestion and digital-twin architectures.

Digital twins: Eclipse Ditto, FIWARE, CesiumJS, PostGIS, GeoServer, and open data models can be combined to create self-hosted pipeline digital twins.

Machine learning and predictive integrity: scikit-learn, XGBoost, PyTorch, pandas, Jupyter, OpenCV, and open corrosion-prediction projects can support risk prediction, anomaly detection, and predictive maintenance.

Drone and satellite inspection: OpenDroneMap, QGIS, GDAL, Rasterio-compatible workflows, OpenCV, and machine-learning frameworks provide an open-source stack for aerial infrastructure monitoring.

Integrity analytics and reporting: Grafana, Apache Superset, Metabase, Jupyter, pandas, PostgreSQL, and DuckDB can provide dashboards, inspection analysis, risk reporting, and management intelligence.

Data engineering: Apache Airflow, Prefect, Apache NiFi, Meltano, dbt Core, Great Expectations, and Pandera can support automated integrity-data pipelines and quality controls.

Industrial data integration: Apache Kafka, Node-RED, MQTT-compatible systems, REST APIs, OPC UA libraries, and custom connectors can integrate operational and engineering information.

Many community projects exist for corrosion prediction, pipeline defect analysis, ILI data processing, predictive maintenance, infrastructure inspection, computer vision, GIS asset management, and industrial digital twins.

Frameworks for building custom systems: Combine the PODS data model with PostgreSQL + PostGIS as the central pipeline asset and spatial data store. Use QGIS and GeoServer for mapping and GIS services, and connect openMAINT, CMDBuild, Atlas CMMS, or ERPNext for maintenance and work-management workflows.

For inspection and integrity analytics, integrate PipelineIntegrity, Python engineering models, pandas, scikit-learn, and XGBoost. Use Apache Airflow, Prefect, or Apache NiFi to automate ILI imports, inspection data processing, GIS synchronization, and scheduled integrity calculations.

For connected infrastructure and digital twins, combine ThingsBoard or OpenRemote, Node-RED, InfluxDB or TimescaleDB, and Eclipse Ditto. Visualize operational and integrity information with Grafana, Apache Superset, Metabase, QGIS, and CesiumJS.

This modular approach can create a powerful, transparent, self-hosted Pipeline Integrity Management platform. Commercial enterprise suites may still provide more mature proprietary ILI integrations, engineering models, regulatory content, specialized fitness-for-service algorithms, certified workflows, industry datasets, and vendor-supported implementation services.

Disclaimer

This is a community-curated list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

Pipeline integrity decisions involve safety-critical engineering, environmental protection, regulatory compliance, and potentially significant operational risks.

Open-source tools and calculation libraries should not be treated as substitutes for qualified engineering review, validated integrity-management processes, or applicable regulatory requirements.

Inspection results, corrosion models, risk assessments, fitness-for-service calculations, and machine-learning predictions must be independently validated before operational decisions are made.

Pipeline operators must comply with applicable standards, regulations, codes, and engineering practices relevant to their jurisdictions and assets.

Self-hosted open-source deployments require appropriate cybersecurity, access controls, data governance, backups, audit trails, validation, and protection of sensitive critical-infrastructure information.

Many open-source projects listed here provide components for GIS, asset management, analytics, IoT, engineering calculations, and data integration rather than complete turnkey replacements for enterprise Pipeline Integrity Management systems.

Made for pipeline operators, integrity engineers, corrosion engineers, inspection specialists, GIS professionals, reliability teams, asset managers, data engineers, and infrastructure technologists who care about transparent asset integrity.
